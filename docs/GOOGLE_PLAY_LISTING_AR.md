## Full description (English)
```
Otolat Rahlatcom (Visafaster) is the app of a private travel and tourism agency. It offers optional paid services for preparing and reviewing visa application files, appointment assistance and travel services.

⚠️ Important disclaimer
This app belongs to a private travel agency. It is not a government app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any embassy, consulate or government entity. We provide optional paid services to prepare and review application files. We do not issue visas and do not guarantee their approval or issuance; the final decision rests solely with the competent official authority.

What the app offers
• Preparing your visa application file and reviewing document completeness against official requirements.
• Helping search for and book available appointments where the service is offered, without guaranteeing that an appointment will be available.
• Secure, encrypted document upload and follow-up on the status of our service.
• Travel, tourism and tour programme services.
• Customer support and official sources for the information shown.

How it works — 4 steps
1) Choose your destination and service.
2) Prepare your file and upload your documents.
3) We review your documents and help search for and book available appointments where offered, without guaranteeing availability.
4) After the review is complete, the client submits the application to the embassy or competent official authority; we can help follow up on the status of our service.

Fees
Our service fees are separate from government fees. Any government fees are subject to the terms and payment mechanism set by the competent official authority. You can apply directly through the official government websites without using our service.
```
